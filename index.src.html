01 — The ask

One frame. A track campaign.

A simple, specific proposal to Handsling Bikes: back Nicola's move to the track with the frame built for it.

After winning the Curlew Cup on the road, Nicola is turning her focus to the track — and she wants to do it on a Handsling.

The request is deliberately modest: a TR3evo endurance frame, not a full build and not wheels. She supplies the engine, the results and the story. Handsling supplies the frame that puts a proven winner on the boards.

  • Raced in 360cycling / partner colours
02 — The story

The comeback

One comeback season to a National A win

Nicola Quaye is a 20-year-old Manx rider who, in her own words, "always got good results, but could never be bothered to do the training." So at the end of sixth form she took a full-time job as a nail technician and stepped away from the sport.

In August last year she got the itch again, went out for one ride, and got hooked. She started training that autumn — completely self-coached, adapting everything to fit herself, driven by the data and her own improvement.

Less than a year later, in her comeback season, she soloed away on the first lap of the 75-mile Curlew Cup and stayed away to take a remarkable maiden National A victory, adding the Queen of the Ryals climbing prize for good measure.

"I started riding again in August last year, and training from around October. But I've never had a coach. I've done it all myself." — Nicola Quaye, Cycling Weekly

04 — Why the track, why now

Built for the boards

The Curlew Cup showed exactly the qualities the track rewards: a huge engine, front-foot aggression, and the nerve to commit early and hold on. Now Nicola is going to hit the track hard.

Endurance track racing — the points race, scratch, elimination and madison — is made for a rider who attacks, reads a race, and can suffer at the front. It is also a discipline where a season of focused work turns raw ability into results fast.

And there is heritage. The Isle of Man is one of British cycling's great track nurseries, a small island that keeps producing world-class track riders. Nicola is the next Manx rider to take to the boards.
